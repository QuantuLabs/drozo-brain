# Data

The model in `data/core_v1` is derived from the **MaleCNS v1.0** connectome (male adult fruit-fly central nervous
system), published by **Janelia FlyEM, Janelia Research Campus**.

- Dataset: MaleCNS v1.0, Janelia FlyEM
- Source page: https://www.janelia.org/project-team/flyem/male-cns-connectome
- License: CC-BY. The dataset's pages state "licensed under CC-BY" and link to the Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International deed (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Paper: "Sexual dimorphism in the complete connectome of the Drosophila male central nervous system",
  Berg et al., bioRxiv 2025, doi:10.1101/2025.10.09.680999
- Attribution: "MaleCNS v1.0, Janelia Research Campus (CC-BY)"

The files in `data/core_v1` (neuron subset, connection lists, synapse counts and signs) are a derived subset of
that dataset and are distributed under the dataset's own terms, with the attribution above. The Apache-2.0 license in
`LICENSE` covers the code of this repository, not the connectome data.

If you use the data, cite the MaleCNS v1.0 release as the dataset authors ask on the source page.
